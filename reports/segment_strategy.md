# Customer Segment Strategy

## High-Value One-Time Buyers

Customers in this segment purchased rarely but spent relatively more per transaction. They are valuable but inactive, so the main strategy is to bring them back with premium-positioned reactivation campaigns.

**Recommended strategy:** Reactivation campaigns with premium positioning  
**Copy angle:** Quality, exclusivity, worth coming back for

---

## Inactive Budget Shoppers

Customers in this segment are low-spending, low-engagement, and inactive. They are likely to respond better to simple, low-risk value offers.

**Recommended strategy:** Win-back discounts, simple value offers, clearance promotions  
**Copy angle:** Affordable, practical, low-commitment

---

## Engaged Budget Shoppers

Customers in this segment are price-sensitive but highly engaged with the brand. They are good targets for sale alerts, bundles, and value-focused campaigns.

**Recommended strategy:** Deal-focused campaigns, bundle offers, personalized sale alerts  
**Copy angle:** Value-driven, timely, deal-oriented

---

## Regular Shoppers

Customers in this segment show moderate and steady purchasing behavior. They are good targets for seasonal recommendations and cross-sell campaigns.

**Recommended strategy:** Cross-sell recommendations, seasonal edits, everyday outfit suggestions  
**Copy angle:** Easy, relevant, everyday style

---

## Loyal High-Value Customers

Customers in this segment are frequent, high-spending, and more recently active. They are the strongest segment for loyalty campaigns, premium recommendations, and early access offers.

**Recommended strategy:** VIP rewards, early access, premium recommendations, loyalty campaigns  
**Copy angle:** Exclusive, personalized, rewarding
